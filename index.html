📜 Official Game Rules

🎮 Mode 1: Single Player

  • Level 1: Range 1 to 100 | 10 Attempts max.
  • Level 2: Range 1 to 1000 | 15 Attempts max.
  • Extreme Hint (Level 2 Only): If your guess is more than 500 away from the secret number, you get a special "Too high!" or "Too low!" hint (triggers only once per round).
  • Standard Feedback: "You guessed it higher" / "You guessed it lower" / "Correct!".

👥 Mode 2: Multiplayer Tournament

  • Supports 2 to 4 Players.
  • Fixed range 1 to 100 with 10 attempts per player.
  • Players take sequential full rounds against their own secret number.
  • The player who solves it in the lowest number of tries wins. Ties are gracefully crowned together!

⚔️ Mode 3: Head-to-Head Duel

  • 1v1 PVP match. Fixed range 1 to 100.
  • Player 1 and Player 2 secretly set numbers for each other.
  • Take alternating turns guessing your opponent's secret number.
  • First player to guess correctly wins instantly!
